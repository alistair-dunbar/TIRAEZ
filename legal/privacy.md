## What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your name and email address.
- Your password, stored only as a one-way scrambled form (a hash). We can't see your password.
- That you confirmed you're 18 or over, when, and how (you told us). We check the age you enter at the moment you enter it, and **we don't keep the number itself**.
- The version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when, and the version of this policy we showed you.
- Whether and when you confirmed your email address. If you ask to change your email, we also keep the new address until you confirm it.
- The single-use links we email to confirm your address or reset your password. We store only a hash of each link's code, with when it expires and whether it has been used.

**When you create videos**

- The photos you upload. We re-save each photo upright and remove the information embedded in it, such as camera details and location.
- How you framed the photo, the details you type (for example a product description), your settings, and the instructions we build from them for the video model.
- The videos and still images generated for you.
- The status and history of each video, including whether it failed and why.

**Credits and payments**

- Your credit balance, and every change to it: credits added, set aside for a video, used, returned after a failed video, and removed by a refund.
- If you buy credits (not switched on yet): the pack, price, status, the payment provider's reference and any refund request. You'd enter card details on the payment provider's page, and they never reach us.

**To keep the service secure**

- A session token in a cookie that keeps you logged in. We store only a hash of it.
- Counts of recent login attempts and requests, held briefly in memory to slow down misuse. They aren't written to our database.
- Technical logs kept by our hosting provider (see "Who receives it").

**If you contact us, report a problem or make a request**

- What you tell us in the form, and the contact email you give, if you give one.

**Emails we send you**

We only send emails your account needs: confirming your address, resetting your password, telling you when your email or password changes, and telling you if someone tries to sign up with your email. They contain no tracking images or tracked links.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts.

## Who receives it, and where it goes

We send your data only to the providers below, and only what each one needs. All of them process it outside Saudi Arabia.

- **Railway Corporation** (hosting). It holds everything the service stores: your account, photos, videos, credit records and reports, plus technical logs. Our servers, database and files are in Railway's US West region (California, United States), and its logs are kept in the United States. Railway acts on our instructions under its data processing agreement. It backs up customer data daily and doesn't publish how long those backups are kept.
- **Resend, Inc.** (account emails). It receives your email address and the text of the account emails listed above. Resend stores this data in the United States, keeps email data for up to 30 days on our plan, and keeps backups for 7 days. It acts on our instructions under its data processing agreement.
- **Features & Labels, Inc. (fal.ai)**, which runs the video model, and the model's developer, **Kuaishou (Kling)**, to whom fal passes the request.
  - They receive the framed copy of your photo and the instructions for the video, never your name, email or account details.
  - fal processes data in the United States and other countries it doesn't name. We haven't been able to confirm where Kling processes it.
  - We ask fal not to keep the request in its history (fal's default would be 90 days), and to delete the uploaded photo and the finished video from its storage after about {{retention.providerFilesHours}} hours.
  - fal's terms let it use anonymised or aggregated data about how its service is used. fal says it doesn't use customers' content to train models, except for models it marks as not yet enterprise-ready. The model we use isn't marked that way.
- **Moyasar**, once card payments are switched on: it processes the payment.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires us to share data.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account, photos and videos:** until you delete them or your account.
- **Photos you uploaded but never used in a video:** deleted automatically after {{retention.unusedPhotosDays}} days.
- **Copies held by the video model provider:** set to expire after about {{retention.providerFilesHours}} hours.
- **Expired sessions:** deleted automatically. **Email links** are deleted a week after they expire. Confirmation links expire after 24 hours, and password reset links after 1 hour.
- **Accounts whose email was never confirmed:** deleted, with everything in them, after {{retention.unverifiedAccountsDays}} days, unless something was bought.
- **When you delete your account:** your name, email, photos, videos, credit history and agreement records are deleted straight away. If you bought credits, the purchase records stay, with your name, email and password removed. {{legal.unresolved.paymentRecordRetention}}
- **Our providers' copies:** Railway's daily backups and logs, and Resend's email data, last until those providers' own retention periods end (see above).

When we delete data, we remove it from our database and delete its files from our storage.

## Under 18s

{{brand}} is only for people aged 18 and over. Asking your age doesn't verify it, and we don't check identity documents.

If someone tells us at sign-up that they're under 18, we don't open an account and we don't store anything they entered.

If an existing account tells us its holder is under 18:
- we immediately stop uploads, video creation and purchases on it;
- we cancel videos not yet sent to the model and return their credits;
- we open a refund request for any credits bought.

The account isn't deleted straight away, so a typing mistake doesn't destroy anyone's work. If it was a mistake, the holder can ask for the restriction to be reviewed; a person checks it before anything changes. Otherwise the account and its data are deleted after {{retention.ageRestrictedDays}} days.

## Security

We protect your data with measures including:
- hashed passwords, session tokens and email links;
- cookies that page scripts can't read;
- encrypted connections;
- checks that only you can open your photos, videos and records;
- limits on repeated attempts.

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.
