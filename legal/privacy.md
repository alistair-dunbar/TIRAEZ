## What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your name and email address.
- Your password, stored only in a one-way scrambled form (a hash). We can't see your password.
- That you confirmed you're 18 or over, when, and how (you told us). We check the age you enter at the moment you enter it, and **we don't keep the number itself**.
- The version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when, and the version of this policy we showed you.
- Whether and when you confirmed your email address. If you ask to change your email, we also keep the new address until you confirm it.
- The single-use links we email you to confirm your address or reset your password. We store only a hash of each link's code, with its expiry time and whether it has been used.

**When you create videos**

- The photos you upload. We re-save each photo upright and remove the information embedded in it, such as camera details and location.
- How you framed the photo, the details you type (for example a product description), your settings, and the instructions we build from them for the video model.
- The videos and still images generated for you.
- Each video's status and history, including whether it failed and why.

**Credits and payments**

- Your credit balance, and every change to it: credits added, set aside for a video, used, returned after a failed video, and removed by a refund.
- If you buy credits (not switched on yet): the pack, price, status, the payment provider's reference and any refund request. You'd enter card details on the payment provider's page, and they never reach us.

**To keep the service secure**

- A session token in a cookie that keeps you logged in. We store only a hash of it.
- Counts of recent login attempts and requests, held briefly in memory to slow down misuse. They aren't written to our database.
- Technical logs kept by our hosting provider.

**If you contact us, report a problem or make a request**

- What you tell us, and the contact email you give, if you give one.

**Emails we send you**

We only send emails your account needs:
- confirming your address;
- resetting your password;
- telling you when your email or password changes;
- telling you if someone tries to sign up with your email.

They contain no tracking images or tracked links.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts on our website.

## How we collect it

- **From you:** what you type into forms, and the photos you upload.
- **Automatically, while you use {{brand}}:** your session, short-lived request counts, and our hosting provider's technical logs.

- **To open an account:** your name, email, a password and your age, plus agreeing to the Terms of Service and confirming you're 18 or over. Without them we can't open an account.
- **To make a video:** a photo and the template's required details. Without them we can't make the video.
- **On the report and request form:** your contact email is optional. Without it we can still act on what you send, but we can't reply.

## Who receives it, and where it goes

We share your data only with the providers below, and only what each one needs. **All of them process it outside Saudi Arabia.**

- **Railway Corporation (hosting).**
  - It holds everything the service stores: your account, photos, videos, credit records and reports, plus technical logs.
  - Our servers, database and files are in Railway's US West region (California, United States). Railway keeps its logs in the United States.
  - Railway acts on our instructions under its data processing agreement. It states that it backs up customer data daily. It doesn't publish how long those backups are kept.
- **Resend, Inc. (account emails).**
  - It receives your email address and the text of the account emails listed above.
  - Resend states that it stores this data in the United States, keeps email data for 30 days on its standard paid plans, and keeps backups for 7 days.
  - It acts on our instructions under its data processing agreement.
- **Features & Labels, Inc. (fal.ai) and Kuaishou (Kling).** fal runs the video model, and passes each request on to the model's developer, Kuaishou (Kling).
  - They receive the framed copy of your photo and the instructions for the video. They don't receive your name, email or account details.
  - fal processes data in the United States and other countries it doesn't name. We haven't been able to confirm where Kling processes it.
  - With each request, we tell fal not to keep it in its request history (otherwise fal keeps it for 90 days). We also set the uploaded photo and the finished video to expire from fal's storage after 24 hours. These are fal's own settings; we can't check from our side that fal deletes on time.
  - fal's terms let it use anonymised or aggregated data about how its service is used. fal states it doesn't use customers' content to train models, except for models it marks as not yet enterprise-ready. The model we use isn't marked that way.
- **Moyasar**, once card payments are switched on: it processes the payment.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires us to share data.

**Transfers outside Saudi Arabia.** Each of these providers processes data for us under its own data processing agreement. We send them only the data described above.

**When you delete a video**, you lose access to it at once. We delete its files and its details, and its photo too unless another of your videos uses it.

**When you delete your account:**
- we log you out everywhere;
- we cancel videos not yet sent to the video model and return their credits;
- we delete your name, email, photos, videos, credit history and agreement records straight away;
- we keep records of purchases, with your name, email and password removed;
- if a video is still with the video model, you'll be asked to wait until it has finished.

**Our providers' copies:**
- **fal:** we set the copies it holds to expire after {{retention.providerFilesHours}}, as described above. We can't check its deletion from our side.
- **Railway:** its backups and logs are kept for its own periods. If a backup were ever restored, our daily deletion run removes anything already past its date again.
- **Resend:** its email records are kept for its own periods.

When we delete data, we remove it from our database and delete its files from our storage.

## Your rights and choices

- **See your data and get a copy:** use "Download your data" on your account page. You'll get a file in a common, readable format.
- **Correct it:** change your name and email on your account page.
- **Delete it:** delete individual videos, or your whole account, on your account page.
- **Other requests,** such as objecting to a use, asking us to restrict it, or withdrawing consent: send a [privacy request](/report?kind=privacy) or email {{business.contactEmail}}. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address.
- **How long we take:** we reply within 30 days. If we need longer, we'll tell you why within those 30 days, and take no more than 30 days more.
- **Complaints:** if you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA) through its National Data Governance Platform, dgp.sdaia.gov.sa.

## Under 18s

{{brand}} is only for people aged 18 and over. Asking your age doesn't verify it, and we don't ask for identity documents.

If someone tells us at sign-up that they're under 18, we don't open an account and don't store what they entered.

If an existing account tells us its holder is under 18, we immediately:
- stop uploads, video creation and purchases on it;
- cancel videos not yet sent to the model and return their credits;
- open a refund request for any credits that were bought.

We don't delete the account on the spot, so that a typing mistake doesn't destroy someone's work. If it was a mistake, the holder can ask us to review the restriction, and a person checks before anything changes. Otherwise we delete the account and its data after {{retention.ageRestrictedDays}}.

## Security

We protect your data with:
- hashed passwords, session tokens and email links;
- cookies that page scripts can't read;
- encrypted connections;
- checks that only you can open your own photos, videos and records;
- limits on repeated attempts.

If a breach could harm you, we'll tell you without undue delay, and we'll notify SDAIA as the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.
