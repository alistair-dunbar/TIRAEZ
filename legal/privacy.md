## What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your name and email address.
- Your password, stored only as a one-way hash. We cannot see your password.
- That you confirmed you are 18 or over, the date you confirmed it and how (you told us). We check the age you enter at the time and **do not keep the number itself**.
- The version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when, and the version of this policy we showed you.

**When you create videos**

- The photos you upload. We re-save each photo upright and remove its embedded information, such as camera details and location.
- How you framed the photo, the details you type (for example a product description and anything to keep or leave out), your settings, and the instructions we build from them for the video model.
- The videos and still images generated for you.
- The status and history of each video, including whether it failed and why.

**Credits and payments**

- Your credit balance and every change to it (credits added, used and returned).
- If online payments are switched on: the pack you bought, the amount, its status and the payment provider's reference. Card details are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach us.

**To keep the service secure**

- A session token in a cookie so you stay signed in. We store only a hash of it.
- Counts of recent sign-in attempts and requests, held briefly in memory to slow down misuse. They are not written to our database.
- Technical logs kept by our hosting provider. {{legal.unresolved.providerAgreement}}

**If you report a problem**

- What you tell us in the report, and the contact email you give, if you give one.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts.

## How we collect it

- **From you**: what you type into forms and the photos you upload.
- **Automatically, while you use {{brand}}**: your session, short-lived request counts and our hosting provider's technical logs.

## What you have to give us

- To open an account you must give your name, email, a password and your age, and agree to the Terms of Service. Without them we can't open an account.
- To make a video you must upload a photo and fill in the template's required details. Without them we can't make the video.
- The contact email on the report form is optional. Without it we can still act on the report, but we can't reply to you.
