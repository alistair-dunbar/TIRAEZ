# Acceptable Use and AI Content Policy

Version {{doc.version}}. Effective {{doc.effective}}.

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to everything you upload, type or generate with {{brand}}.

## Do not use {{brand}} for

- Anything unlawful, or to help anyone break the law.
- **Any sexual content involving children**, or content that sexualises anyone under 18. We report it to the authorities where required.
- Sexual or intimate images of a real person without their consent.
- Impersonating a real person or organisation, fraud, scams, or videos made to deceive people about something real, such as fake endorsements, events or product claims.
- Content that threatens, harasses, bullies or promotes violence or hatred against people.
- Infringing someone else's copyright, trade mark, image rights or privacy, for example using another company's product photos or logo as if they were yours.
- Uploading photos of people, or personal information about them, unless you have the right to use them in this way.
- Misleading advertising, including claims about products that you cannot support.
- Anything our video model provider forbids in its [acceptable use policy](https://fal.ai/legal/acceptable-use-policy), including deepfakes, using someone's likeness without their consent, and infringing intellectual property.

## Do not interfere with the service

- Do not try to get around our limits, safeguards or access controls, or to reach other people's photos or videos.
- Do not use automated tools to create accounts or videos, or to overload the service.
- Do not share your account or resell access to {{brand}}.

## Before you publish

Videos are made by an AI model and can change or invent details. Check each video before you use it, make sure any claims in your advertising are accurate, and follow the rules of the platforms you post on and any laws that require AI-generated content to be labelled. Some platforms, including TikTok, require AI-generated ads to be labelled.

## Reporting a problem

If you see something made with {{brand}} that breaks this policy, or that uses your photos, your likeness or your brand without permission, tell us using the [report form](/report) or at {{business.contactEmail}}. Include a link or the video number if you have it.

## What we do with reports

- Every report goes into a review queue that the {{brand}} team checks.
- We do not automatically review every upload before it is processed, but the video model's provider may refuse some requests.
- If we find a breach, we may remove the content, suspend or close the account, and report illegal content to the authorities where the law requires it.
- If we act on your account and you think we got it wrong, contact {{business.contactEmail}} and a person will look at it again.
- We don't promise round-the-clock moderation: reports are reviewed by the people who run {{brand}}.
