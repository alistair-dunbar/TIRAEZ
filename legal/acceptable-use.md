# Acceptable Use and AI Content Policy

Version {{doc.version}}. Effective {{doc.effective}}.

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to everything you upload, type or create with {{brand}}.

## Don't use {{brand}} for

- Anything unlawful, or to help anyone break the law.
- **Any sexual content involving children**, or content that sexualises anyone under 18. We report it to the authorities where the law requires.
- Sexual or intimate images of a real person without their consent.
- Fraud, scams, or impersonating a real person or organisation.
- **Deceptive deepfakes:** videos made to mislead people about something real, such as fake endorsements, fake events, or a real person appearing to say or do something they didn't.
- Content that threatens, harasses or bullies people, or promotes violence or hatred against them.
- Infringing someone else's copyright, trade mark, design or privacy, for example using another company's product photos, packaging or logo as if they were yours.
- Uploading photos of identifiable people, or personal information about anyone, unless you have the right to use them in this way.
- Misleading advertising, including claims about a product that you can't support.
- Anything our video model provider forbids in its [acceptable use policy](https://fal.ai/legal/acceptable-use-policy). That includes deepfakes, using someone's likeness without their consent, and infringing intellectual property.

## Don't interfere with the service

- Don't try to get round our limits, safeguards or access controls, or to reach other people's photos, videos or accounts.
- Don't abuse our payment or credit systems, for example by using stolen cards or exploiting errors to get credits you haven't paid for.
- Don't use automated tools to create accounts or videos, or to overload the service.
- Don't share your account, or resell or give others access to {{brand}}.

## Before you publish a video

Videos are made by an AI model and can change or invent details. Before you use a video:
- check it carefully;
- make sure any claims in your advertising are accurate;
- follow the rules of the platforms you post on, and any law that requires AI-generated content to be labelled. Some platforms, including TikTok, require AI-generated ads to be labelled.

## Reporting a problem

If something made with {{brand}} breaks this policy, or uses your photos, your likeness or your brand without permission, tell us. Use the [report form](/report) or email {{business.contactEmail}}, and include a link or the video number if you have it. You don't need an account to report.

## What we do with reports

- **Who reviews them.** The people who run {{brand}} review every report. We don't offer round-the-clock moderation.
- **No automatic screening.** We don't automatically screen every upload before it's processed. The video model's provider may refuse some requests.
- **What we may do.** If we find a breach, we may remove the content, suspend or close the account, and report illegal content to the authorities where the law requires it.
- **Asking us to look again.** If we act on your account and you think we got it wrong, email {{business.contactEmail}} and a person will look at the decision again.
