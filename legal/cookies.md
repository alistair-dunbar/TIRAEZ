# Cookies and Browser Storage

Version {{doc.version}}. Effective {{doc.effective}}.

{{brand}} uses only what is needed for features you ask for. We do not use analytics, advertising or other tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party scripts, so there is nothing to opt in or out of.

## What we store on your device

| Name | Type | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| sid | cookie | Keeps you logged in. Pages cannot read it, and it is only sent over a secure connection. | 30 days, or until you log out |
| video-draft | session storage | Keeps your photo choice and details while you prepare a video, so a refresh doesn't lose them. | Until you close the tab |
| catalogue-url | session storage | Remembers your template search and filters when you go back to the catalogue. | Until you close the tab |

Session storage stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## If this changes

If we ever add optional cookies, such as analytics, we will ask for your permission first and let you change your choice at any time. Until then, no choice is needed.
