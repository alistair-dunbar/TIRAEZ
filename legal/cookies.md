# Cookie Policy

Version {{doc.version}}. Effective {{doc.effective}}.

This policy lists everything {{brand}} stores on your device, and why.

We use only what's needed to provide features you ask for. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't load scripts from other companies. That's why we don't show a cookie banner: there's nothing optional to accept or reject.

## What we store on your device

| Name | Type | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| sid | cookie | Keeps you logged in. Scripts on the page can't read it, and it's only sent over a secure connection to {{brand}}. | 30 days, or until you log out |
| video-draft | session storage | Keeps your photo choice and details while you prepare a video, so a page refresh doesn't lose them. | Until you close the tab |
| catalogue-url | session storage | Remembers your template search and filters for when you go back to the templates. | Until you close the tab |

Session storage stays in your browser. It isn't sent to us.

## Your choices

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time.
- Deleting the **sid** cookie logs you out.
- Blocking it means you can't log in.
- Blocking session storage means a refresh may lose a video you're preparing. The rest of the site still works.

## If this changes

If we ever want to use optional cookies or storage, such as analytics, we'll ask for your permission first. Optional ones will be off until you agree, and you'll be able to change your choice at any time from a link in the footer. Until then, no choice is needed.
