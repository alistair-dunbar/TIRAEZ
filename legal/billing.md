## Your right to cancel

If you're a consumer, the Saudi E-Commerce Law lets you cancel within 7 days of buying, as long as you haven't received or benefited from the service. For credits, that means a pack with **no credits used**: within 7 days of buying it, you can cancel it and get your money back. That law can treat a business buying from us as a consumer too.

Once a video has been made for you, the service has been delivered, so the 7-day right doesn't cover it.

## Refunds

{{legal.unresolved.unusedCreditRefunds}}

**How to ask:**
1. On your account page, find the purchase and choose "Request a refund". You can also email {{business.contactEmail}} with the email on your account and the date of the purchase.
2. A person reviews every request.
3. If we refund a purchase, its unused credits are removed from your balance, and the money goes back through the payment provider to the card you paid with.
4. We'll tell you the outcome by email.

## Closing your account

Before you delete your account, ask for a refund of any purchase you're entitled to get back. Once the account is deleted, its credits can't be used.

You keep every right that consumer protection law gives you, including any right to cancel or to a refund that the law provides. Nothing on this page takes those rights away.
