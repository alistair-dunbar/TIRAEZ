## How credits work

- Videos are paid for with credits that you get in advance. {{pricing.perVideo}}
- Before you create a video, the review step shows how many credits it will use and your balance.
- When you press "Generate video", the credits are **set aside** for that video:
  - if the video is made, they're **used**;
  - if it fails (for example, the video model refuses the request or doesn't finish), they're **returned** to your balance automatically, and the video's page says so.
- Your account page shows how many credits are available, set aside, used, returned, and removed by refunds.
- When a video expires after 90 days, or you delete it, only the file goes. The credits it used and your remaining balance don't change.

## Cancelling and refunds

How a refund is handled depends on what has happened to the credits.

**A pack with no credits used, within 7 days of buying it.** If you're a consumer, the Saudi E-Commerce Law lets you cancel within 7 days, as long as you haven't received or benefited from the service. A pack with no credits used meets that condition, so you can cancel it and get your money back. Under that law, a business buying from us can count as a consumer.

**Credits you haven't used, in a pack you've partly used, or after 7 days.** {{legal.unresolved.unusedCreditRefunds}}

**Videos that failed.** You don't need to ask: their credits are returned to your balance automatically.

**Videos that were made.** Once a video has been made, that service has been delivered, so the 7-day right to cancel doesn't apply to its credits. If a video you received is faulty (for example, the file won't play or doesn't match the format you chose), tell us. You keep any right the law gives you when a service isn't provided with reasonable care.

**Our mistakes.** If we charge you wrongly, for example twice for the same purchase, we'll refund the difference.

## How to ask for a refund

1. On your account page, find the purchase and choose "Request a refund". You can also email {{business.contactEmail}} with the email on your account and the date of the purchase.
2. A person reviews every request and tells you the outcome by email.
3. If we refund a purchase, its unused credits are removed from your balance, so they can no longer be used. The money goes back through Moyasar to the card you paid with. {{legal.unresolved.refundTiming}}

## If an account is restricted or closed

- **You delete your account:** credits in it can't be used afterwards, so ask for any refund you're entitled to first.
- **An account is restricted because its holder said they're under 18:** we open a refund request for credits that were bought.
- **We close an account without a good reason under our terms:** we refund the credits paid for and not used.
