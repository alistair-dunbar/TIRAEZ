# Social media template library

50 copy-and-paste post templates: 10 niches × 5 types (promotional, educational, social proof,
behind-the-scenes, urgency/offer).

**How to use**
- Replace everything in `[BRACKETS]`. Examples inside brackets (`e.g. …`) show the kind of detail that works.
- Specific numbers are what make these persuasive. If you don't have the number, find it; don't delete the line.
- Each template works as an Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or LinkedIn caption. "Pair with" suggests the photo or video.
- Use real quotes and real results only, and get the person's permission first.
- Niches 7, 8 and 10 (clinics, law, finance) are regulated in most countries. The templates avoid
  guarantees and include short disclaimers; check your local advertising rules before posting.

## 1. Specialty Coffee Shops

### 1.1 Promotional: new coffee on bar
```
New on bar: [ORIGIN], [PROCESS, e.g. washed / natural].

Tastes like [NOTE 1], [NOTE 2], and a finish of [NOTE 3].
We roasted it [X] days ago, which is right when it peaks.

Try it as [BREW METHOD] this week. [PRICE].

📍 [ADDRESS] · open [HOURS]
```
Pair with: a close-up of the pour, the bag in the background.

### 1.2 Educational: fix your home brew
```
Your coffee at home tastes bitter for one of three reasons:

1. Water too hot. Aim for [92–96°C], not boiling.
2. Grind too fine. Go one step coarser.
3. Beans too old. Check the roast date, not the expiry date.

Fix one at a time and taste the difference.

Still stuck? Bring your beans in and we'll dial them in with you. Free.
```
Pair with: a three-slide carousel, one fix per slide.

### 1.3 Social proof: the regular
```
"[CUSTOMER QUOTE, one or two sentences in their own words]."

[FIRST NAME], regular since [YEAR]. Orders the [DRINK] every [DAY].
We've made it about [NUMBER] times. Still not bored.

What's your usual? Tell us below.
```
Pair with: a photo of the customer at the counter (with permission).

### 1.4 Behind the scenes: before opening
```
[TIME] a.m. Doors are still locked.

[NAME] has already pulled [NUMBER] shots and thrown [NUMBER] of them away.

That's dialing in: adjusting the grind until today's espresso tastes the way it
should, because yesterday's setting never works twice.

You get the next shot. The good one.
```
Pair with: a short clip of shots being pulled and tasted.

### 1.5 Urgency: limited lot
```
[NUMBER] bags. That's all we got of [COFFEE NAME].

It's a [LOT TYPE, e.g. micro-lot] from [FARM / REGION]. When it's gone, it's gone
until next harvest.

On bar and on the shelf from [DAY].
[PRICE] per [SIZE]. Limit [X] per person so more of you get to try it.

```
Pair with: the bags stacked and counted on the counter.
